The records of the ambient `<List>` as a dated activity list, one entry per record in list order. The timeline fetches nothing: put it inside a `<ReferenceManyField>` or a `<List>`, whose sort sets the order. Each entry shows the `messageSource` text, an icon, and optionally the `userSource` and the time. The time is relative ("2 hours ago"), from `Intl.RelativeTimeFormat`, with the full date in the tooltip and the ISO date in `dateTime`; an entry with no valid date leaves it out. `iconSource` picks an icon from `icons`, and an entry with no match shows a clock. It is computed at render and does not tick while the page stays open.
